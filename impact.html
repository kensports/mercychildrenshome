Our Journey

From 2020 to Today

2020

Mercy Children's Home Opens

Alex Ssengonzi opens his own home to the first 5 vulnerable children in Kasanda Bukuya. No funding, just faith and love. The journey begins.

2021

Growing to 15 Children

Word spreads. 15 children are now in the home. First international donors discover the home. School fees paid for the first full term. A small garden is started for food.

2022

25 Children — GoFundMe Launched

The home grows to 25 children. The GoFundMe campaign is launched. All children are enrolled in school. Medical care is provided for the first time to all children.

2023

30 Children — First Volunteers Arrive

30 children in the home. Shelter improvements made. First international volunteers visit from the USA. 3 full meals per day become consistent. Donor community grows worldwide.

2024

32 Children — A Full House of Love

The home reaches 32 children. 100,000+ meals served since opening. A dedicated professional website is launched. Newsletter brings together a global community of supporters.

2026

Today — 6 Years Strong

210,000+ meals served. 18 school terms funded. 32 children with shelter, food, education and love. With your help, we will secure permanent land and give every child a forever home.
