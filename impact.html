Our Journey

From 2020 to Today

2020
Mercy Children's Home Opens

Alex opens his home to the first 5 vulnerable children in Kasanda Bukuya, Mityana District. No funding, just faith and love. The journey begins.

2021
Growing to 15 Children

Word spreads. 15 children are now in the home. First international donors discover the home through YouTube. School fees are paid for the first term. A small garden is started for food.

2022
25 Children & First Website

The home grows to 25 children. The GoFundMe campaign is launched. The YouTube channel grows. All children are enrolled in school. Medical care is provided for the first time to all.

2023
30 Children & Shelter Improvements

30 children are now in the home. Shelter improvements are made. First international volunteer visits. 3 full meals per day become consistent. Donor community grows worldwide.

2024
32 Children — A Full House of Love

The home reaches 32 children. 100,000+ meals served since opening. A dedicated website is launched. The prayer wall and newsletter bring together a global community of supporters.

2026
Today — 6 Years Strong

210,000+ meals served. 18 school terms funded. 32 children with shelter, food, education and love. With your help, we will reach our $10,000 goal and secure the next chapter for every child.
