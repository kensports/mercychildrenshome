Our Mission

Giving Every Child
a Future Worth Living

Our mission is simple: no child in our care will go to bed hungry, grow up unloved, or be denied an education. We exist to break the cycle of poverty through faith, love and action.

Four Pillars

How We Serve These Children

Food & Nutrition

Three nutritious meals every single day. No child at Mercy Home goes to bed hungry. We grow vegetables on site and buy protein weekly from local markets.

Education

Every child attends school. We pay school fees, buy uniforms and provide books. Education is treated as a right, not a privilege — it is the key to their future.

Shelter & Safety

A safe, clean home where every child sleeps on a bed with a blanket. We are currently fundraising to purchase permanent land so this home is secure forever.

Spiritual Growth

We raise children with faith, values and purpose. Weekly church, prayer, and Bible study teach them who they are and give them a foundation that lasts a lifetime.

Where Your Money Goes

100% Goes to the Children

Alex personally oversees every shilling. No salaries are taken from donations. We are fully transparent and open to audit at any time.
