Our Mission

What We Do
Every Day

Six pillars of holistic care — shelter, food, education, healthcare, faith, and future skills — that give 32 children a real chance at life.

The 6 Pillars

What We Provide Every Day

We provide holistic care that nurtures every aspect of a child's development — body, mind, and spirit.

01

Safe Shelter

Many of our children come from situations of neglect and uncertainty. Here, they find more than just a roof — they find a place where they are safe, loved, and finally at peace.

02

Education

Every child deserves a chance to learn and dream. We provide school fees, uniforms, books, and support — giving each child the opportunity to build a brighter future.

03

Nutrition

For many of our children, regular meals were never guaranteed. Today, we provide three nourishing meals a day — restoring their strength, health, and hope.

04

Healthcare

We ensure every child receives the medical care they need — from routine checkups to urgent treatment — so they can grow up strong and healthy.

05

Spiritual Care

Beyond physical needs, we nurture the heart. Through faith, love, and guidance, we help each child discover their value, purpose, and identity in God.

06

Future Skills

As they grow, we equip our children with practical skills and confidence — preparing them for independent, meaningful lives beyond the home.

Our Impact

Why This Mission Matters

Uganda has an estimated 2.7 million orphaned children. Many grow up without shelter, education, or proper nutrition — robbed of any chance at a future.

At Mercy Children's Home, we are doing something about it — one child at a time, in the village of Kalaata, Bukuya. And every single day, we see what love, faith, and support can do.

Love & Faith
Our Vision

"Building God's Kingdom, One Child at a Time"

We believe every child who comes through our doors carries the seed of something great. Our vision is to nurture that seed — with love, faith, and opportunity — until they flourish into the leaders Uganda needs. Because when a child is given a chance, they do not just survive. They soar.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

2020

Mercy Children's Home is Founded

Out of a deep calling to serve vulnerable children in Kasanda Bukuya, Mercy Children's Home opened its doors for the first time — welcoming its first children with love and faith.

2021

Growing Our Family

More children found their way to the home as word spread through the community. Programs expanded to include structured education support and regular healthcare access.

2022–2023

Reaching the World

Global supporters began partnering with the mission — from individuals to churches around the world. Every contribution helped provide more meals, more education, and more hope.

Today

32 Children & Growing

Today we care for 32 children and continue to grow. The mission remains the same — love every child, serve every need, and never stop believing in a better future.
