Our Story

From One Man's Faith
to a Family of 32

2020 — The Beginning
One Man, Five Children, Zero Funding
Ssengonzi Alex opened his home to 5 orphaned children with nothing but faith. He saw children going to bed hungry in his community and could not walk away.
2021 — Growing
A Family Grows to 15
Word spread internationally. Sponsors from the USA began giving monthly support. The home grew to 15 children — all receiving meals, clothing and love every day.
2022 — Education
School for Every Child
Every child was enrolled in school. Global sponsors made it possible for every child to have uniforms, books and paid school fees. Education is non-negotiable at Mercy Home.
2025 — Today
32 Children & The Land Campaign
Today 32 children call Mercy Home their family. The urgent need now is permanent land — they live on rented ground and need a secure home they can truly own forever.
Our Values

What We Stand For

Faith

Every decision rooted in the belief that every child is created in God's image and deserves dignity, love and a future.

Family

We are not an institution — we are a family. Every child belongs here. Real home, real love, real future.

Education

Education breaks the cycle of poverty. Every child attends school — no exceptions, no excuses, ever.

Transparency

100% of donations go to the children. Open to audit. Alex personally accounts for every shilling received.

Community

We partner with local churches, families and international sponsors to build a global village around these children.

Excellence

Every child deserves the best — nutrition, healthcare, education, spiritual growth and emotional care every day.
