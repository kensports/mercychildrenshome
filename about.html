Who We Are

What We
Stand For

Mercy Children's Home was founded with one powerful belief — that every child, no matter their background, deserves to be loved, sheltered, fed and educated.

We are not just a children's home. We are a family. Every child who arrives broken leaves empowered. Every story of pain becomes a story of hope.

"We didn't start with money. We started with love. And love has been enough."

— Alex Ssengonzi, Founder · Mercy Children's Home
  • Daily food and nutrition for all 32 children

  • Full school fees and education support

Meet the Founder

The Heart
Behind Mercy

Alex Ssengonzi grew up understanding poverty. When he saw children on the streets of Uganda with nowhere to go, he opened his own home — with no funding, no grants, just love and faith.

Today he leads a team of dedicated volunteers and staff to care for 32 children in Kasanda Bukuya. His dream is to secure permanent land so these children can have a forever home.

"Every child I see on the street reminds me why I can never stop. Until every child has a home, I will keep going."

Our Values

What We
Believe

01

Every Child Matters

No child is too broken, too small or too forgotten. Every child at Mercy Children's Home is seen, known and loved.

02

Love Before Resources

We started with love before we had money. Love is still our greatest resource and the foundation of everything we do.

03

Full Transparency

Every donation is accounted for. We share exactly how funds are used — because donors deserve to know their impact.

04

Community First

We work with local Ugandan communities, churches and schools — because sustainable change starts from within.

05

Education Always

We believe education is the most powerful tool against poverty. Every child at Mercy goes to school — no exceptions.

06

Faith Driven

Our work is rooted in faith and scripture. We believe God's love for children calls us to action every single day.

"Religion that God our Father accepts as pure and faultless is this: to look after orphans and widows in their distress."
— James 1:27
